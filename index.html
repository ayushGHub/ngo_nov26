Our approach

Three ways we change what school can be

We start where the need is sharpest and build outward — grounded in the village, powered by technology, and designed to scale.

01

Beginning at the grassroots

Our first centre in Rohtas district brings learning opportunities closer to children in surrounding villages.

02

Digital empowerment

We are developing access to computers, reliable internet and digital-library resources so students can explore more ways to learn.

03

Cluster school initiative

Our cluster-school approach is designed to connect nearby villages with shared learning facilities shaped around local needs.

Who are we?

Education is the catalyst for change

LEAF is a Section 8 non-profit based in Sasaram, Bihar. We work to make quality education and learning resources more accessible to children in rural communities.

At our Rohtas centre, students learn in class, explore books and digital resources, and take part in early-childhood activities.

Our work grows from the needs of children and families in the surrounding villages. Learn more about our registration and the documents we publish.
