Support our cause

Your gift supports learning resources, early-childhood education, computer access and the staff who support students at our centre in Rohtas, Bihar.

Why support LEAF?

Your support reaches the classroom

Donations help meet current needs at our centre and in nearby villages, including learning materials, connectivity, classroom facilities and staff support.

Every gift helps make learning more accessible to children in rural Rohtas.

Donations through this website are pooled across LEAF's priorities. For offline donations or questions, email contact@letseducateall.org.
