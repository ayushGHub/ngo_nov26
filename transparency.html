About the foundation

The Let's Educate All Foundation is incorporated under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 (CIN U80900BR2021NPL051236), pursuant to rule 19(2) of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014.

Section 8 companies in India are formed specifically to promote charitable objects — including education — and must apply any profits towards those objects rather than distributing them to members.
